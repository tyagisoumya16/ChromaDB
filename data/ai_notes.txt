Artificial Intelligence is the field of building systems that perform tasks that normally require human intelligence.
Machine Learning is a subset of Artificial Intelligence. Machine Learning systems learn patterns from data.
Deep Learning is a subset of Machine Learning that uses neural networks with multiple layers.
Natural Language Processing focuses on enabling computers to process and understand human language.
Generative AI refers to AI systems capable of generating new content such as text, images, audio, and code.
Retrieval-Augmented Generation combines information retrieval with generative AI. A retrieval system finds relevant information and an LLM uses that information to generate an answer.
Vector databases store vector representations of data and allow similarity-based retrieval.
